## EN
> **Short answer:** It depends on the glue. White/PVA glue has a minimum application temperature on its data sheet (for example, one PVA TDS lists 8°C) — check yours and keep the workshop and materials above it. Hot melt doesn't "dry", it cools: on cold board or in a cold draft it can set before it bonds. Bring materials up to production-floor temperature and keep cold air off the glue line. [1][2][3][4]

| Phenomenon | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| White glue problems in a cold workshop | Below the PVA's minimum application temperature | Check the TDS minimum (e.g. 8°C on one sheet) and heat the workshop/materials above it; store glue as the TDS specifies (that sheet: 10–30°C) | [1] |
| Hot melt sets before pressing, peels off clean (common in winter) | Cold substrate cools the glue too quickly | Ensure substrate and adhesive are at the right temperature; substrates brought straight in from outside should warm up first | [2][3] |
| Hot melt fails near fans, doors, AC | Cold drafts on the glue line or nozzles | Block cold air flow; Henkel notes ambient temperature and air flow "can shock hot melt adhesive" | [3][4] |
| Open time too short in winter | Glue cooling too fast | Raise nozzle/hose temperature no more than 5°C (9°F) at a time, within the TDS range | [3] |

**How this relates to Zhishang machines:** We make both [cold-glue machines](/products/zs-s088e-4/) — brochure: "can dispense white glue, yellow glue and all-purpose glue" — and [hot-glue machines](/products/zs-s088r-4/) — "can dispense hot melt and pressure-sensitive adhesive". The temperature rules above apply to both.

## ZH
> **简答：** 看用的是哪种胶。白乳胶（PVA）的技术资料（TDS）会写最低施工温度（例如有一份写 8°C），车间和材料要保持在这个温度以上。热熔胶不是「干」，而是冷却凝固；纸板太冷或有冷风，胶可能还没粘上就凝了。材料先回到车间温度，胶线避开冷风。[1][2][3][4]

| 现象 | 可能原因 | 解决办法 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 冷车间里白胶出问题 | 低于 PVA 最低施工温度 | 查 TDS 的最低温度（如某款 8°C），车间和材料升到以上；按 TDS 储存（该款 10–30°C） | [1] |
| 热熔胶没压合就凝了，一撕就掉（冬天常见） | 工件冷，胶冷却太快 | 工件和胶都要在合适温度；从室外直接拿进来的材料先回温 | [2][3] |
| 靠近风扇、门口、空调处开胶 | 冷风吹胶线或胶嘴 | 挡住冷风；Henkel 指出环境温度和气流会「冲击」热熔胶 | [3][4] |
| 冬天开放时间太短 | 胶冷却太快 | 胶嘴、胶管温度每次最多调高 5°C（9°F），不超出 TDS 范围 | [3] |

**和至尚设备的关系：** 至尚有[冷胶划胶机](/zh/products/zs-s088e-4/)（画册：「可以划白胶、黄胶、万能胶」）和[热胶划胶机](/zh/products/zs-s088r-4/)（「可以划热熔胶，压敏胶」），以上温度要求对两类都适用。
