## EN
> **Short answer:** A common cause is too much water-based glue. LD Davis, a maker of gelatin glue for rigid boxes, notes that extra glue doesn't make a stronger bond, and because gelatin glue is applied as a liquid, the additional moisture can cause the board to warp. Apply the amount your adhesive maker specifies (LD Davis: 1–2 mils for its protein glues), measured with a mil depth gauge, at the correct temperature. [1]

| Phenomenon | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board bows after wrapping | Too much liquid (gelatin/water-based) glue adding moisture | Apply the maker's specified amount; measure with a mil depth gauge (LD Davis: 1–2 mils) | [1] |
| Turn-ins lift as well | Too little or too much glue | Same — check against the adhesive's technical information | [1] |
| Glue sets too fast or burns | Wrong glue temperature | Run at the specified temperature (LD Davis gelatin: 140–150°F) | [1] |
| Only some boxes affected | Inconsistent compression on the box machine | Check plunger/form compression settings with the equipment maker | [1] |
| Still warping after the above | Other factors (e.g. facility environment) | LD Davis notes the facility environment can also play a role; consult your adhesive supplier | [1] |

**Test bond quality (LD Davis):** pull a turn-in; a "good fiber tear" means proper adhesion. [1]

## ZH
> **简答：** 常见原因是水性胶用多了。精品盒明胶厂 LD Davis 指出，胶多不等于粘得牢；明胶是液态涂布，多出的水分会让纸板翘曲。按胶水厂规定的量上胶（LD Davis 蛋白胶 1–2 mil），用 mil 厚度规测量，并控制好胶温。[1]

| 现象 | 可能原因 | 解决办法 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 裱完纸板弯 | 液态胶（明胶、水性胶）太多，水分多 | 按规定胶量，用 mil 规测（LD Davis 1–2 mil） | [1] |
| 包边也翘开 | 胶太少或太多 | 对照胶水技术资料调整 | [1] |
| 胶凝太快或烧焦 | 胶温不对 | 按规定温度（LD Davis 明胶 140–150°F） | [1] |
| 只有部分盒子出问题 | 成型机压合不一致 | 与设备厂核对压头、成型压力设置 | [1] |
| 以上都查过仍翘 | 其他因素（如车间环境） | LD Davis 提到车间环境也有影响，联系胶水供应商 | [1] |

**检测粘接（LD Davis）：** 撕开包边，带纸纤维撕裂说明粘接良好。[1]

**和至尚设备的关系：** 至尚[划胶机](/zh/products/)按程序路径上胶，划胶宽度 3–10mm，有[冷胶](/zh/products/zs-s088e-4/)和[热胶](/zh/products/zs-s088r-4/)两类机型。
