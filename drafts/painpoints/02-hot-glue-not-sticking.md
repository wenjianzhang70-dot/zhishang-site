## EN
> **Short answer:** Weak hot melt bonds usually come from glue that is too cool to wet the surface, too little glue, poor pressing, a hard-to-bond surface (film-laminated or UV-coated paper), or a cold substrate that sets the glue too early. Fix temperature, glue amount and pressing first, then check whether the adhesive suits the material. [1][2][3]

| Phenomenon | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrap peels off clean, no fiber on the glue | Glue temperature too low, poor wetting | Raise temperature within the TDS range | [1][3] |
| Bond only in spots | Not enough glue | Increase pressure or nozzle size; check flow is even | [1][2] |
| Some boxes hold, others don't | Inconsistent pressing | Make sure pressing time and pressure are sufficient and consistent | — |
| Fails on laminated / UV / coated paper | Surface hard to bond; adhesive not matched | Choose an adhesive for that surface (Arkema's guide notes MPO-type hot melts suit PE-coated paper better than EVA) and re-test whenever the paper supplier changes | [3][2] |
| Glue sets before pressing | Cold substrate, short open time | Bring substrates to production-floor temperature (Bostik notes this is common in winter); review open time | [2][3] |

**Quick test (from LD Davis, for wraps):** pull a glued turn-in apart; paper fiber tearing means good adhesion, a clean peel means a glue or pressing issue. [4]

**How this relates to Zhishang machines:** Our [hot-glue](/products/zs-s088r-4/) and [cold-glue](/products/zs-s088e-4/) dispensing machines apply glue automatically along a set path. The brochure states one in-line machine "saves two people's gluing work." Adhesive choice and pressing still need to be matched to your materials.

## ZH
> **简答：** 热熔胶粘不牢，常见原因是胶温低、润湿不够，上胶量少，压合不到位，面纸难粘（覆膜、UV），或者工件太冷让胶提前凝固。先查温度、胶量和压合，再看胶水是否适合这种材料。[1][2][3]

| 现象 | 可能原因 | 解决办法 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 面纸一撕就掉，胶上没纸纤维 | 胶温低，润湿不够 | 在 TDS 范围内提高温度 | [1][3] |
| 只有局部粘住 | 上胶量不足 | 加大压力或胶嘴，检查出胶是否均匀 | [1][2] |
| 有的粘得住有的不行 | 压合不均 | 确保压合时间和压力足够且一致 | — |
| 覆膜纸、UV 纸粘不住 | 表面难粘、胶型不对 | 按材料选胶（Arkema 指南：PE 覆膜纸用 MPO 类比 EVA 合适），换纸张供应商要重新测试 | [3][2] |
| 还没压合胶就凝了 | 工件冷、开放时间短 | 材料回到车间温度（Bostik 指出冬季常见）；核对开放时间 | [2][3] |

**快速检测（LD Davis）：** 撕开包边，撕下来带纸纤维说明粘接好；整片干净剥离说明胶或压合有问题。[4]

**和至尚设备的关系：** 至尚[热胶](/zh/products/zs-s088r-4/)和[冷胶](/zh/products/zs-s088e-4/)划胶机按设定路径自动上胶，画册写随线机型「一台机节省两个人划胶」。胶水型号和压合仍需按材料匹配。
