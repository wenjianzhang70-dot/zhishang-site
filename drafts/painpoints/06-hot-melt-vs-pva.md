## EN
> **Short answer:** They behave differently. Water-based glues are applied as a liquid, and excess glue adds moisture that can warp board (LD Davis, on gelatin glue); PVA also has a minimum application temperature on its TDS. Hot melt sets by cooling, so it is sensitive to cold substrates and drafts, and its heated tank needs maintenance to prevent char. The right choice depends on your materials, climate and process — ask your adhesive supplier. [1][2][3][4][5]

| Factor | White / PVA (water-based) | Hot melt | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it sets | Water-based emulsion, applied wet | Applied molten, sets as it cools | [1][2] |
| Cold sensitivity | Minimum application temperature on TDS (e.g. 8°C) | Cold substrate or drafts can set it before bonding | [1][3][4] |
| Moisture into board | Excess liquid glue can cause warping (LD Davis, gelatin) | — | [2] |
| Equipment care | Wash out before glue dries (per TDS) | Heated tank; filters, monthly purge, cleaning every six months | [1][5] |

**How this relates to Zhishang machines:** We build both lines. Cold-glue models (e.g. [ZS-S088E-4](/products/zs-s088e-4/), [ZS-S088C](/products/zs-s088c/), [ZS-S088D](/products/zs-s088d/)) "can dispense white glue, yellow glue and all-purpose glue". Hot-glue models (e.g. [ZS-S088R-4](/products/zs-s088r-4/), [ZS-S088D-4](/products/zs-s088d-4/)) "can dispense hot melt and pressure-sensitive adhesive"; the brochure says the three-axis cabinet hot-glue model "can replace double-sided tape, saving the labor and time of peeling release paper."

## ZH
> **简答：** 两者特性不同。水性胶是液态涂布，胶多了带进的水分会让纸板翘曲（LD Davis 讲明胶），PVA 的 TDS 上还会写最低施工温度。热熔胶靠冷却凝固，怕工件冷和冷风，加热胶箱要定期保养防碳化。选哪种要看材料、气候和工艺，可以问胶水供应商。[1][2][3][4][5]

| 对比项 | 白胶 / PVA（水性） | 热熔胶 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 固化方式 | 水性乳液，湿涂 | 熔融涂布，冷却凝固 | [1][2] |
| 怕冷 | TDS 有最低施工温度（如 8°C） | 工件冷、有冷风会提前凝固 | [1][3][4] |
| 带进纸板的水分 | 液态胶过量会翘曲（LD Davis，明胶） | — | [2] |
| 设备保养 | 胶干前用水清洗（按 TDS） | 加热胶箱；过滤网、每月排胶、每半年清箱 | [1][5] |

**和至尚设备的关系：** 两条产品线都有。冷胶机型（[ZS-S088E-4](/zh/products/zs-s088e-4/) 等）「可以划白胶、黄胶、万能胶」；热胶机型（[ZS-S088R-4](/zh/products/zs-s088r-4/) 等）「可以划热熔胶，压敏胶」；三轴柜式热胶「可以刮热胶，替代双面胶，节省撕离型纸的人工和时间」。
