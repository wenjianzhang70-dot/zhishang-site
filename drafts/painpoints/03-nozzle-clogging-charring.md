## EN
> **Short answer:** Clogged nozzles are usually caused by char: glue degraded by heat, oxygen, contamination and time. Dust and debris in the tank, long idle periods at full temperature and a near-empty tank all speed it up. Smoking or darkening glue is a sign of overheating or oxidised glue. Keep the tank covered and at least half full, use standby (setback) temperature when idle, filter, and replace charred glue. [1][2][3]

| Phenomenon | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nozzle blocks, bead skips | Char particles from degraded glue | Use tank and inline filters; purge through the tank drain port monthly | [1] |
| Black specks in the bead | Char / contamination in tank | Clean out the tank interior every six months; replace compromised adhesive | [1][3] |
| Glue darkens, smokes, smells | Temperature too high or glue oxidised | Check thermostat, reduce temperature, use setback or turn off when idle; replace glue and cover reservoir | [3] |
| Char builds faster than expected | Tank lid open, dust falling in; tank run near-empty | Keep lid closed; keep tank at least half full | [2] |

## ZH
> **简答：** 胶嘴堵塞多数是碳化造成的：胶在热、氧、杂质和时间作用下变质。胶箱进灰尘杂质、空闲时长时间保持高温、胶箱太空，都会加快碳化。胶变黑、冒烟，说明温度过高或胶已氧化。盖好胶箱并保持至少半满，空闲用待机降温，加装过滤网，碳化的胶要换掉。[1][2][3]

| 现象 | 可能原因 | 解决办法 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 胶嘴堵、断胶 | 碳化颗粒 | 用胶箱和管路过滤网；每月从排放口排胶 | [1] |
| 胶线里有黑点 | 胶箱碳化、杂质 | 每半年清理胶箱内部；变质的胶换掉 | [1][3] |
| 胶变黑、冒烟、有味 | 温度过高或胶氧化 | 检查温控、降温，空闲时待机降温或关机；换胶并盖好胶箱 | [3] |
| 碳化特别快 | 胶箱没盖、掉灰；胶箱太空 | 盖好胶箱，保持至少半满 | [2] |

**和至尚设备的关系：** 画册原话：「划胶枪嘴终身包修，包换（以旧换新）。划胶嘴为我司自主研发品牌，有发明专利和实用新型专利。」日常保养仍按以上要求。见[划胶机产品](/zh/products/)。
