ZS-YH4040 Inline Rigid Box Bubble Pressing MachineAsk about this machine

Features

  1. Presses box bottoms, blister inserts and paper trays; for phone boxes, tablet boxes, perfume boxes and more.
  2. Presses one or two at a time with switchable modes for higher efficiency.
  3. The machine can be placed at any position according to gluing requirements.
  4. Single head can press 100-1200 pieces; double head can press 2000-2400 pieces.
