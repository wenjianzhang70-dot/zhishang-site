Custom Automated Meal Box Gluing Machine

A custom-built, fully enclosed automated gluing line for paper meal boxes. Boxes travel along the conveyor while the multi-axis heads apply cold glue exactly where it is needed, replacing a manual gluing station with a steady, repeatable result.

Products
Paper meal boxes
Glue
Cold glue
Build
Enclosed cabinet · conveyor · multi-axis heads

This machine is a custom-built, fully enclosed automated gluing line for paper meal boxes. Boxes travel along the conveyor while the multi-axis gluing heads apply cold glue exactly where it is needed, replacing a manual gluing station with a steady, repeatable result.

Like all our non-standard machines, it is built to order: size, glue type, and line layout are matched to your product. Tell us about your boxes and glue, and we will confirm the layout for your line.

Features

  1. Fully enclosed cabinet line for meal box gluing
  2. Conveyor-fed: boxes move through the line automatically

  4. Custom-built: size, glue type, and layout matched to your product
  5. Replaces a manual gluing station with a steady, repeatable result

Applications

For meal box and food container producers who need to replace manual gluing with an automated line: boxes are glued as they pass on the conveyor, then move to the next process.
