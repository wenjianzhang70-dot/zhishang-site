Features

  1. Suited to large cases and recessed card insert products; high gluing precision; one machine replaces 2 workers.
  2. Large gluing size, strong versatility across glued products, high flexibility and easy operation.
  3. The machine can be placed at any position according to gluing requirements.
  4. Applies white glue, yellow glue and universal glue to box bottoms, cases and flat recessed card insert surfaces.
  5. Fully upgraded control system: faster speed, more stable performance.
