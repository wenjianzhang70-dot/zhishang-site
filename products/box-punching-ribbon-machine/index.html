Automatic Box Punching Machine & Ribbon Attaching Machine

Two machines that work alone or as a line: the first punches ribbon holes in the box, the second feeds the ribbon and bonds it inside. Used on wine, jewelry, mooncake, cosmetic, luxury and watch boxes.
