Features

  1. Suitable for any workshop assembly line or fully automatic production line, and can be used across the line; flexible and convenient.
  2. Separate assembly line with start-stop function, helping to improve efficiency and ensure product quality.
  3. In semi-automatic use, a wooden board is placed on the machine runner and handled manually.
  4. For lid-and-base boxes, book-shaped boxes, wine boxes, box-in-box, mobile phone boxes, cosmetics boxes, jewelry boxes and more.
  5. Flat gluing; for sub-sensitive glue and hot melt glue; glue width 5-12mm (adjusted with different gun nozzles).
