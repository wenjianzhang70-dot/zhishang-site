Molds

Sanshi Precision Mold is Zhishang Technology's affiliated mold shop, set up in 2020. It designs and machines non-standard molds and fixtures, and precision parts for automation equipment.

What we make

  • Precision machining of automation parts
  • Machine accessories
  • Non-standard mold design and manufacturing

We build molds to order, for example: four-piece corner forming sets, hexagonal bubble-press molds, lid-and-base box forming machine molds, paper-tube curling molds, tube cutting and punching molds, blister press molds for phone boxes, split molds, corner molds, stamping molds and fixtures for automation equipment. We hold the mounting-hole layouts of the common forming machines on the market, so molds fit straight on.
