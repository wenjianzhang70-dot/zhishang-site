Hot Melt vs White (PVA) Glue for Rigid Boxes

They behave differently. Water-based glues are applied as a liquid, and excess glue adds moisture that can warp board (LD Davis, on gelatin glue); PVA also has a minimum application temperature on its TDS. Hot melt sets by cooling, so it is sensitive to cold substrates and drafts, and its heated tank needs maintenance to prevent char. The right choice depends on your materials, climate and process — ask your adhesive supplier. [1][2][3][4][5]
