Glue Not Drying or Sticking in Cold Weather

It depends on the glue. White/PVA glue has a minimum application temperature on its data sheet (for example, one PVA TDS lists 8°C) — check yours and keep the workshop and materials above it. Hot melt doesn't "dry", it cools: on cold board or in a cold draft it can set before it bonds. Bring materials up to production-floor temperature and keep cold air off the glue line. [1][2][3][4]

Sources: Everbond General Purpose PVA TDS (PDF; a woodworking PVA, cited for the TDS minimum-temperature convention)；Bostik, 7 reasons hot melt won't stick；Bostik/Arkema THERMOBOND Troubleshooting Guide (PDF)；Henkel, Preventing stringing in hot melt adhesives

Hot melt sets before pressing, peels off clean (common in winter)Cold substrate cools the glue too quicklyEnsure substrate and adhesive are at the right temperature; substrates brought straight in from outside should warm up first [2][3]
