Zhishang S088 Gluing Machines: Inline, Cabinet, Dual-Station, and 3-Axis vs 4-Axis Selection Guide

Selecting among the 12 Zhishang S088 gluing and spraying machines depends on three engineering boundaries: (1) line layout, matching inline conveyor units (600x600 mm), large-table cabinets (900x600 mm), dual-station alternating tables (450-540 mm x 2), or spray coaters [ZS]; (2) axis count, choosing 3-axis for flat cases or 4-axis for box bottoms plus four inner walls [ZS]; and (3) cold glue (1.4-2.3 kW) versus hot melt replacing double-sided tape (1.4-5.3 kW) [ZS] [ADH].

The three engineering divides in the S088 series

Every machine in the Zhishang S088 series shares a programmable CNC motion control system, single-phase 220V AC 50Hz power, 0.6 MPa working air pressure, and patented glue nozzles covered by a lifetime repair and trade-in replacement warranty [ZS]. The 12 configurations separate cleanly along three production variables:

  • 1. Line architecture (Inline vs Cabinet vs Dual-Station vs Spray): Inline units (ZS-S088E-4, ZS-S088R-4, ZS-S088R-3, and the Inline 4-Axis Cold Glue Sprayer) sit directly on a manual conveyor or automatic lid-and-base box line and handle boxes up to 600 x 600 x 130 mm, saving two manual gluing operators per machine [ZS]. Standalone cabinet units (ZS-S088C, ZS-S088C-4, ZS-S088D, ZS-S088D-4) expand the bed to 900 x 600 mm for oversized book-style boxes, folding boxes, and large cases. Dual-station units (ZS-S088B, ZS-S088B-2-R, ZS-S088A, ZS-S088A-R) split the table into two alternating zones (450-540 x 300-420 mm x 2) so an operator loads one fixture while the head glues the other.
  • 2. Axis count (3-Axis flat vs 4-Axis bottom + side walls): A 3-axis machine moves linearly in X, Y, and Z for flat work such as cases, book covers, box bottoms, and recessed card inserts. A 4-axis machine adds rotational head articulation so it can glue the box bottom, four vertical inner walls, and flat faces simultaneously in a single pass [ZS].
  • 3. Adhesive delivery (Cold glue bead vs Hot melt bead/scraping vs Spray coating): Cold-glue models run water-based white PVA, yellow glue, and universal glue at 1,400 to 2,300 W. Hot-glue models include heated tanks and hoses (1,400 to 5,300 W) to run hot melt and pressure-sensitive adhesive (PSA), replacing double-sided tape and eliminating manual liner peeling [ZS]. Spray models (ZS-S088P and the inline 4-axis sprayer) atomize cold glue into an even film (3-20 mm width on S088P) for leather-box covering fabrics and broad box panels.

Real trade-offs and physical boundaries to check before ordering

Every S088 configuration is optimized for a specific box geometry and glue chemistry. Account for these four boundaries when planning your line:

  • Inline width cap (600 x 600 x 130 mm): ZS-S088E-4, ZS-S088R-4, and ZS-S088R-3 accept workpieces up to 600 x 600 x 130 mm. Unfolded book-style box covers, apparel boxes, or wine presentation cases up to 900 x 600 x 150 mm must run on a cabinet model (ZS-S088C, ZS-S088C-4, ZS-S088D, or ZS-S088D-4), or on a custom non-standard machine (such as the 1,200 x 1,200 mm ZS-FB-019).
  • 3-axis cannot glue vertical inner walls: 3-axis models (ZS-S088D, ZS-S088D-4, ZS-S088A, ZS-S088R-3) are restricted to top-down flat paths. If your process glues the four vertical inner walls of a lid-and-base box together with the bottom, specify a 4-axis machine.
  • Hot-melt power draw and char prevention: While cold-glue cabinet and inline models draw 2,000 to 2,300 W, 4-axis hot-melt models (ZS-S088R-4, ZS-S088C-4) draw 5,300 W to heat the tank, hose, and gun. Hot-melt systems require inline/tank filtration, monthly drain purging, and six-month tank cleaning to prevent carbon char blockages [ADH]. Read our Hot Melt vs White (PVA) Glue Guide and 12-Point Hot-Melt Ordering Checklist for full maintenance details.
  • Adhesive temperature resistance for export shipments: Affiliated company Jiuzhou Shunchi supplies VOC-tested hot melt adhesives (Q-8313D softening point 76-86 C; Q-8501BA softening point 97-107 C; working temperature 160-180 C) and universal glues (DC-805, DC-402, DC-806, SC-906, DC-206) [ZS]. The brochure notes that these hot-melt grades have a service temperature range of roughly -10 to 50 C, so export orders exposed to extreme container heat or sub-zero transit should verify adhesive grade selection in advance [ZS] [PVA].

Step-by-step selection workflow

  1. Measure your largest and smallest unfolded case or assembled box (L x W x H in mm) and enter them into the Rigid Box Machine Matcher & Plant Calculator.
  2. Check where the glue line sits: flat surfaces only (cases, box bottoms, card inserts) fit a 3-axis model; inner vertical box walls plus bottom require a 4-axis model.
  3. Match the workstation to your floor layout: inline conveyor pass-through (up to 600 x 600 mm) for continuous assembly lines, cabinet (up to 900 x 600 mm) for large formats, or dual-station (up to 540 x 420 mm x 2) for high-mix manual loading.
  4. Decide between bead dispensing (3-10 mm or 5-12 mm structural lines) and surface spraying (3-20 mm on ZS-S088P or inline 4-axis spraying for leatherette and thin wraps).
  5. Select cold glue (1.4-2.3 kW, water washout) or hot melt / PSA (1.4-5.3 kW, fast tack to replace double-sided tape), and review the 12-Point Hot-Melt Ordering Checklist before requesting a quote.

Frequently asked questions

When do I need a 4-axis S088 gluing machine instead of a 3-axis model?

Choose a 4-axis model (ZS-S088E-4, R-4, C, C-4, B, or B-2-R) whenever you need to glue the box bottom, four inner side walls, and flat rim faces in a single cycle. A 3-axis model (ZS-S088D, D-4, A, A-R, R-3, or S088P) moves in X, Y, and Z only and is built for flat cases, box bottoms, and recessed card inserts.

What is the maximum box size for inline S088 models versus cabinet models?

Inline S088 models (ZS-S088E-4, R-4, R-3, and the inline 4-axis cold glue sprayer) handle up to 600 x 600 x 130 mm. For oversized book-style boxes, folding boxes, or cases between 600 mm and 900 mm long, use a cabinet model (ZS-S088C, C-4, D, or D-4) with a 900 x 600 mm working area.

How do I choose between cold glue and hot melt S088 models?

Cold-glue models dispense water-based white PVA, yellow glue, and universal glue at 1,400 to 2,300 W and clean up with water before drying. Hot-glue models dispense hot melt and pressure-sensitive adhesive at 1,400 to 5,300 W to set in seconds or replace double-sided tape, and require regular filter and tank maintenance to prevent char.

When should I choose glue spraying (ZS-S088P or inline sprayer) over bead gluing?

Choose glue spraying when covering leather-box fabrics or thin wrap materials that need an even surface coating (3 to 20 mm spray width on ZS-S088P) without raised glue ridges showing through. Choose bead dispensing (3 to 10 mm or 5 to 12 mm line width) for structural box walls, bottoms, card inserts, and double-sided tape replacement.

What electrical and air supply do S088 gluing machines require?

All S088 gluing and spraying machines run on single-phase 220V AC 50Hz power and 0.6 MPa compressed air. Rated power ranges from 1,400 W on dual-station and spray units to 5,300 W on 4-axis inline and cabinet hot-melt units.
